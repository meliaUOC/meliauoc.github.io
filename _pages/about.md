## Research Interests

My research interests are broadly in improving technologies for **Sustainability**, including **Passive RF Sensing**, and **Internet of Things**.

If you are interested in the above topics, please take a look at [the following available research lines](http://www.uoc.edu/portal/en/escola-doctorat/linies-recerca/linies-nit/wireless-networks/index.html).

I am always open to research collaborations!

## Bio

I was born in Maó, [Menorca](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=en&geocode=&q=Ma%C3%B3&sll=39.882606,4.32312&sspn=0.138576,0.439453&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=Mah%C3%B3n,+Balearic+Isles,+Balearic+Islands,+Spain&t=h&ll=39.912896,4.10202&spn=0.554058,1.234589&z=10&iwloc=A), a beautiful island in the middle of the Mediterranean Sea. I did my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Telecommunication Engineering at [Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya](http://epsc.upc.edu/) in Barcelona, and the M.Sc. Thesis at the Mobile Communications department of [Aalborg Universitet](http://kom.aau.dk/) (Denmark).

After that period I worked as an RFID Project Engineer at AIDA Centre Company, while doing an M.Sc. in Information and Network Society at [UOC](http://www.uoc.edu). In 2008 I received a grant to join the [Internet Interdisciplinary Institute](http://in3.uoc.edu) (IN3) as a graduate student, completing the Ph.D. Thesis in 2011. In 2011 I joined the [DTIC](http://www.upf.edu/dtic/en/), [Universitat Pompeu Fabra](http://www.upf.edu/), as Postdoctoral Lecturer and Researcher, and in 2012 I was Visiting Researcher at [Palo Alto Research Center (PARC, a Xerox Company)](http://www.parc.com). In 2014 I joined [EIMT](http://www.uoc.edu/portal/en/estudis_arees/informatica_multimedia_telecomunicacio/index.html), [Universitat Oberta de Catalunya](http://www.uoc.edu/), where I am currently Associate Professor. In 2022 I was Fulbright Visiting Scholar at the [Auto-ID Lab](https://autoid.mit.edu/), [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://www.mit.edu/) (MIT).

Besides my academic activity, I co-founded the NGO Telecos Sense Fronteres (telecomms without borders), with the goal to improve the health and education systems of developing countries by means of communication technologies. Our last project was a [communications system](http://telecossensefronteres.blogspot.com/) between health centers in south Nicaragua's Atlantic Region.
